## Design Context

### Users
Long-time Hux Blog readers, developers arriving from GitHub, and visitors following old deep links. They should immediately understand that the site is a preserved archive and have a calm, obvious path to Hux's current website without losing access to the original content.

### Brand Personality
Quietly confident, editorial, and nostalgic. The experience should retain the original Hux Blog's thoughtful developer-writer personality, with concise bilingual communication and no marketing-heavy treatment.

### Aesthetic Direction
A restrained archival notice that belongs to the original light, photographic Jekyll theme: ink-toned typography, the site's existing teal accent, fine rules, and compact asymmetric composition. Avoid generic announcement bars, floating promotional cards, gradients, excessive rounding, or attention-seeking motion.

### Design Principles
- Preserve the article or page as the visual priority; archival context should be present but never intrusive.
- Keep English and Chinese equally legible and concise, with a clear hierarchy rather than duplicated visual weight.
- Reuse the site's existing typography, spacing rhythm, and teal accent so additions feel native.
- Prefer durable, dependency-free HTML and CSS with accessible focus states, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion support.
- Adapt deliberately on small screens while keeping the link to the current site visible.
